About SHI

SHI goes beyond manufacturing.

PT. Seo Heung Indoraya was established in March 2011 in Karawang — originally as PT. Seo Heung Trading Indonesia — to support the procurement of Chang Shin Indonesia. Today the company runs three lines: MRO distribution, chemical raw material supply, and installation & maintenance.

We are part of Seoheung Co. Ltd, founded in 2004, and of the Changshin Group — operating across Korea, China, Vietnam and Indonesia under the SHC Indonesia brand.

Procurement Model

One purchase order instead of twenty.

A factory buys consumables, spare parts, safety gear, chemicals and construction material — usually from dozens of separate vendors. Being a Total Solution Provider means SHI carries that fragmentation instead of the customer.

39 trading licences 10,000+ MRO items Bonded stock in Cikarang Sourcing across 4 countries

What the customer gains

  • One purchase order, one invoice, one point of contact.
  • Lower unit prices, because volume is consolidated across customers.
  • Shorter lead time — stock already sits in the bonded centre at Cikarang.
  • Fewer staff hours spent on sourcing, comparing and chasing vendors.

What the vendor gains

  • Access to a wider market without building a sales organisation.
  • They concentrate on making the product; we handle distribution and service.
  • One counterparty and predictable orders — lower admin and collection risk.

PDPLB — Bonded Logistics Center

Import duty stays deferred until the material leaves the warehouse.

Together with PT. RLC Indonesia, SHI operates a bonded logistics facility — Pusat Logistik Berikat — in Jababeka, Cikarang. Manufacturers can hold imported material inside Indonesia, close to the line, without paying duty and import tax up front.
